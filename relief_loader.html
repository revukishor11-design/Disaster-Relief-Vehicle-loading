Disaster Relief Vehicle Loader

Enter vehicles, relief items and destinations. The greedy loader picks the most valuable items that fit weight and volume limits, then compares itself with random loading.

Vehicles
Relief items (priority 1-10; need per person = units one person requires)
Destinations (urgency 1-5)

How the algorithm works

1. Each destination gets a score = urgency x people / (1 + distance/100). Vehicles are assigned one by one to the destination with the highest score per vehicle already sent there.
2. Every item unit has a value = priority² x destination urgency (squaring makes water and medicine dominate when space is short).
3. Cost of one unit = its larger share of the vehicle's weight or volume limit. Items are sorted by value / cost (greedy for a multi-constraint bounded knapsack, O(n log n)).
4. Units are loaded in that order, never exceeding stock, the destination's remaining need, or either capacity.
5. The baseline loads items in random order with the same limits and the same vehicle-to-destination assignment, averaged over 300 runs.
